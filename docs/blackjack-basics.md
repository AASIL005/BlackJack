# Blackjack Basics

## Goal
Get a hand value closer to 21 than the dealer without going over 21.

## Card values
- Number cards are worth their number.
- J, Q, and K are worth 10.
- An Ace is worth 1 or 11, whichever is more useful without busting.

## How a round works
1. The player and dealer receive two cards.
2. Choose **Hit** to take another card.
3. Choose **Stand** to keep the current hand.
4. Going over 21 is a bust and ends the hand.
5. After the player stands, the dealer draws according to the game's rules (commonly until reaching at least 17).
6. The hand closer to 21 wins, unless the player busts.

## Quick examples
- 10 + 7 = 17: a valid hand.
- 10 + 8 + 5 = 23: a bust.
- Ace + 9 = 20: the Ace counts as 11.
